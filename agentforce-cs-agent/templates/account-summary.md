### Overall Customer Sentiment

In 2-3 sentences:

- classify the customer as HAPPY, NEUTRAL, or AT RISK
- explain why using Timeline Notes / Activity Sentiment
- reference specific activity dates and subjects as supporting context

End with a separate bold status line using exactly one of:

- **Overall: HAPPY ✅**
- **Overall: NEUTRAL 🟡**
- **Overall: AT RISK 🔴**

If Timeline evidence is insufficient:

**Overall: NOT ENOUGH DATA**
