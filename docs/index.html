CSM Adoption Agent

Spot the right adoption opportunities

  • Scans a CSM's book of business across usage data, Success Plans, CTAs, and customer conversations to identify adoption gaps and opportunities.
  • Recommends relevant adoption goals or lets CSMs define their own, from re-engaging inactive users to encouraging new feature adoption.

Go from insight to customer-ready outreach

  • Gives CSMs the ability to review and approve suggested messages before drafting them in connected email tools.
  • Works directly within a CSM's AI workspace through Gainsight CS MCP, without requiring a separate Gainsight interface.

Start with one prompt

Prep the next call without installing anything.

Paste this into Gainsight Copilot and replace the customer name. The same prompt is in the repository, with thirteen more beside it.

Gainsight Copilot
Prep me for my next call with [customer name]. Tell me where the account stands (health, renewal, ARR, stage), who I should talk to, the open risks and CTAs, what happened recently on the timeline, the landmines to avoid, and 3 questions I should ask. If Staircase is connected, include sentiment and what the customer said on recent calls. If it is not connected, say so. If a value is missing, say so. Do not invent numbers.
