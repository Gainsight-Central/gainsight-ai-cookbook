# Customer story and business review

Before a QBR or EBR. One narrative, then the deck outline built from it. Replace [customer name]. This does not change the record.

Copy the prompt below into Gainsight Copilot.

```text
Tell me the full customer story for [customer name] ahead of a business review.
Write one narrative: where the relationship stands, what they are trying to achieve, what has landed, and what is stuck.
Use Gainsight for health, renewal, CTAs, the success plan, and the timeline.
If Gainsight PX, Skilljar, Community, or Support is connected, fold in product adoption, certification, community engagement, and support, and name the source. If one is not connected, say so and leave it out.
If Staircase is connected, add sentiment and what the customer has said. If it is not connected, say so.
Then give a short exec briefing: what leadership will ask, and the talking points.
Then outline the QBR or EBR deck from that narrative, slide by slide, using PX adoption and Skilljar certification numbers where they exist.
Do not change anything in Gainsight.
If a value is missing, say so. Do not invent numbers.
```
