# Customer story

Before a business review, when you want one narrative rather than a 90-day outline. Replace [customer name]. This does not build a deck or change the record.

Copy the prompt below into Gainsight Copilot.

```text
Tell me the full customer story for [customer name], the one I would take into a business review.
Write one narrative: where the relationship stands, what they are trying to achieve, what has landed, and what is stuck.
Use Gainsight for health, renewal, CTAs, the success plan, and the timeline.
If Gainsight PX, Skilljar, Community, or Support is connected, fold that in and name the source. If one of them is not connected, say so and leave it out.
If Staircase is connected, add sentiment and what the customer has said. If it is not connected, say so.
Close with a short exec briefing: what leadership will ask, and the talking points.
Do not create a deck or change anything in Gainsight.
If a value is missing, say so. Do not invent numbers.
```
