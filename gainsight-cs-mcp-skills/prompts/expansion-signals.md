# Expansion signals

Before a growth conversation on one account. Replace [customer name]. This does not create an opportunity, a CTA, or an email.

Copy the prompt below into Gainsight Copilot.

```text
Where is the expansion opportunity on [customer name]?
Separate what the customer has asked for from readiness signals in usage, learning, community, and the success plan. Name the source of each signal.
Note any competitor mention and whether it changes the timing.
Give up to three plays, who they involve, and whether to move now or validate first. If nothing is supported, say so.
If Staircase is connected, include sentiment and quotes. If it is not connected, say so.
Do not create a CTA, an opportunity, or an email.
If a value is missing, say so. Do not invent numbers.
```
