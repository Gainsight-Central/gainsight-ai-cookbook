# Exec meeting prep

For an exec walking into a customer meeting, or a CSM briefing the exec who joins. Replace [customer name]. This stays in the chat.

Copy the prompt below into Gainsight Copilot.

```text
Brief me for an exec meeting with [customer name].
Cover sentiment and what is behind any recent drop, product usage, open support issues, the renewal date and ARR, and the stakeholders in the room and where each one stands.
Tell me what their leadership will push on, and give me the talking points.
Keep it to one screen.
If Staircase is connected, use sentiment and what the customer said on recent calls. If it is not connected, say so.
Do not post or change anything in Gainsight.
If a value is missing, say so. Do not invent numbers.
```
