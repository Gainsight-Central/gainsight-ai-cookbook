# My command center

The start of a CSM's day, across the book. No customer name needed.

Copy the prompt below into Gainsight Copilot.

```text
Build my command center for today across my book.
Today's actions: rank the signals and to-dos in one list. For each, say whether a CTA is already in play or nobody is on it, and give the one next step.
Churn radar: accounts renewing in the next 90 days, with renewal date, ARR, and health side by side.
Top account: for the account at the top, say why it surfaced, who is in the buying group and where each person stands, a suggested next step, and a comparable win from another account if one exists.
If Staircase is connected, use it to explain why an account surfaced, and move an account up when recent calls sound worse than the health score. If it is not connected, say so.
Do not send email or create, close, or update a CTA until I approve.
If a value is missing, say so. Do not invent numbers.
```
