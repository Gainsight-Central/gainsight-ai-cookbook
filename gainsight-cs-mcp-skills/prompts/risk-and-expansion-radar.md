# Risk and expansion radar

The whole book on one view, for a CS leader or an exec. No customer name needed.

Copy the prompt below into Gainsight Copilot.

```text
Show me the risk and expansion radar for my book. If I lead a team, use the team's book.
Split ARR into three groups: safe, at risk, and expansion still on the table.
Use the health labels and risk CTAs configured in Gainsight, not fixed cutoffs, and say which rule you used.
For each group, give the total ARR, the account count, and the accounts that matter most, with the reason.
If Staircase is connected, use sentiment and recent conversations, and flag accounts where sentiment disagrees with health. If it is not connected, say so.
Do not change anything in Gainsight.
If a value is missing, say so. Do not invent numbers.
```
