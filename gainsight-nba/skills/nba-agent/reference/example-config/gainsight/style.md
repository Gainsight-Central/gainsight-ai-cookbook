# Style (example: Gainsight)

These emails are customer-facing adoption nudges from a CSM. Write them the way a good CSM writes, not the way marketing writes.

## Voice
- Write "you," not "we." Lead with the benefit and the so-what.
- Say what the reader can now do that they could not before, and why it is worth two minutes.
- Short and skimmable, one screen. Length and structure come from the plugin default (90 to 150 words), not from this file.
- Borrow the words the customer, CS, and Support actually use. Do not invent product-speak.
- One clear ask. One link or next step, not three.

## Grounding
- Every claim traces to a fact retrieved in the data and ground steps. No invented usage numbers, dates, names, or ARR.
- Reference the real signal plainly ("your team has 40 licensed seats and 8 have logged in this month") only if you actually pulled that number. Otherwise speak generally.
- Mark anything inferred. If you cannot ground it, cut it.

## Subject line
- Specific and honest, not clickbait. Name the value or the feature, not "Quick question."

## Format
- Greeting by first name if you have it, else a neutral opener.
- One ask.
- Sign as the CSM (use the owner from Staircase/CS if available, else a placeholder the CSM fills).

## Hard bans (house style)
- No em dashes. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses.
- Banned words: delve, landscape, synergy, leverage, robust, streamline, cutting-edge.
- No "not just X, it's Y" constructions. State the positive claim.
- No hollow windups ("I wanted to reach out," "I hope this finds you well," "Just checking in"). Open with the point.
- One idea per sentence. Active voice.
